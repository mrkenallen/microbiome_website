Overnight oats with kefir, chia and raspberries

Five minutes the night before gets you a creamy, tangy breakfast that's ready when you are. Kefir does the soaking, so there's no cooking.

Quick Look

Stir 1 cup oats, 1 cup kefir, ½ cup milk, 2 tbsp chia, ½ tsp vanilla and a pinch of cinnamon; fold in ½ cup raspberries. Chill 6 hr or overnight. Top with ½ cup raspberries and 2 tbsp walnuts.

Prep Time

5 minutes

Waiting Time

At least 6 hours, or overnight, in the refrigerator

Gut-health note

Kefir is a fermented food, and oats, chia and raspberries bring fiber. An early Stanford study found that adults who ate more fermented foods for 10 weeks had more diverse gut microbes, though experts say the benefits aren't yet established. Source

Good to know

Kefir contains live cultures. If you're immunocompromised, check with your doctor before eating live-culture foods. New to fiber? Start with a half portion.
