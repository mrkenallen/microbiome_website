Red lentil dal with spinach

Red lentils cook down to a creamy base in about 20 minutes. A quick pan of cumin, onion, ginger and tomato does the rest.

Ingredients for Red lentil dal with spinach
To serve 4Ingredients
1 cupRed lentils, rinsed
3 cupsWater
½ tGround turmeric
1 TOil or ghee
1 tCumin seeds
1Onion, diced
3 clovesGarlic, minced
1 TFresh ginger, grated
1 tGround coriander
½ tGaram masala
PinchChili flakes
14.5 oz canDiced tomatoes
4 cupsBaby spinach
1 TLemon juice
To serveSalt, cilantro, plain yogurt and brown rice

T = tablespoon · t = teaspoon

Prep

  1. 01

    Rinse the lentils in a strainer until the water runs mostly clear.

  2. 02

    Dice the onion, mince the garlic and grate the ginger.

Prep step photo
Prep step photo
Prep step photo

Steps

  1. 01

    Simmer the lentils, water and turmeric 15–20 minutes, stirring now and then, until soft.

  2. 02

    Meanwhile, heat the oil and sizzle the cumin seeds 30 seconds.

  3. 03

    Add the onion and cook 6–8 minutes.

  4. 04

    Add the garlic, ginger, coriander, garam masala and chili; cook 1 minute.

  5. 05

    Add the tomatoes and simmer 5 minutes.

  6. 06

    Stir the tomato mixture into the lentils. Add the spinach and stir until wilted.

  7. 07

    Season with lemon juice and salt. Serve with cilantro, a spoon of yogurt and brown rice.
