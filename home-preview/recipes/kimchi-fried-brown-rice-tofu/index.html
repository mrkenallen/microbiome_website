Kimchi fried brown rice with tofu

Day-old brown rice, crisp tofu and kimchi two ways: half cooked into the rice for depth, half folded in at the end so it stays crunchy.

Ingredients for Kimchi fried brown rice with tofu
To serve 3Ingredients
14 ozExtra-firm tofu, pressed and cubed
1 TCornstarch
2 TNeutral oil, divided
2 clovesGarlic, minced
3Scallions, whites and greens separated
1 cup + 2 TKimchi, chopped, plus kimchi juice
1 cupFrozen peas and carrots
3 cupsCooked brown rice, chilled
1 TLow-sodium soy sauce
1 tGochujang (optional)
1 tToasted sesame oil
To serve3 fried eggs and sesame seeds (optional)

T = tablespoon · t = teaspoon

Prep

  1. 01

    Press the tofu, then cut it into bite-size cubes.

  2. 02

    Mince the garlic. Slice the scallions, keeping the whites and greens apart.

  3. 03

    Chop the kimchi and save 2 tablespoons of its juice.

Steps

  1. 01

    Toss the tofu with the cornstarch.

  2. 02

    Heat 1 tablespoon oil over medium-high heat and crisp the tofu, 8–10 minutes. Set aside.

  3. 03

    Add the remaining oil, the garlic, scallion whites and half the kimchi; cook 2 minutes.

  4. 04

    Add the peas and carrots, then the rice. Press it into the pan and cook 4–5 minutes until crisp in spots.

  5. 05

    Stir in the soy sauce, gochujang and kimchi juice. Return the tofu.

  6. 06

    Off the heat, fold in the remaining kimchi, sesame oil and scallion greens.

  7. 07

    Top with fried eggs and sesame seeds, if using.

Step photo

Good to know

Cooking kimchi kills its live cultures, which is why half goes in at the end.
