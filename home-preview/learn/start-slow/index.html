Start slow, stay safe

By Ken Allen · [DATE] · Article in progress

The short version

  • Add fiber gradually and drink water with it. People with IBS, IBD or strictures, those taking MAOIs, anyone watching sodium, and people who are immunocompromised should take extra care with high-fiber and fermented foods.
