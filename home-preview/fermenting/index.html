Safety basics

  • Keep it under the brine. Vegetables must stay completely submerged so the right bacteria grow and air stays out. (Clemson Extension (HGIC))
  • Mind the temperature. At 70–75°F, sauerkraut is fully fermented in about three to four weeks; at 60–65°F it may take six weeks. (Penn State Extension)
  • Know when to toss it. Don't taste a ferment that has mold on the surface, a slimy texture or a bad smell. Discard the batch if there's a lot of visible mold. (Penn State Extension)
  • Refrigerate when it's done. Once fermenting is finished, store the food in the refrigerator or can it properly. (UC Agriculture and Natural Resources)
  • Fermented drinks can contain alcohol. When sugar and yeast ferment, a drink like kombucha can reach 0.5% alcohol or more. If you avoid alcohol, keep that in mind with home-brewed drinks. (U.S. Alcohol and Tobacco Tax and Trade Bureau)

For tested recipes and full guidance, see the National Center for Home Food Preservation.
