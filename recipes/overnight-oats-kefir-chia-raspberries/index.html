Overnight oats with kefir, chia and raspberries

Five minutes the night before gets you a creamy, tangy breakfast that's ready when you are. Kefir does the soaking, so there's no cooking.

Steps

  1. Stir the oats, kefir, milk, chia, vanilla and cinnamon together in a jar or bowl.

  2. Fold in half the raspberries.

  3. Cover and refrigerate at least 6 hours or overnight.

  4. Stir, then top with the remaining raspberries and the walnuts.

Gut-health note

Kefir is a fermented food, and oats, chia and raspberries bring fiber. An early Stanford study found that adults who ate more fermented foods for 10 weeks had more diverse gut microbes, though experts say the benefits aren't yet established. Source · Read more

Good to know

Kefir contains live cultures. If you're immunocompromised, check with your doctor before eating live-culture foods. New to fiber? Start with a half portion.
